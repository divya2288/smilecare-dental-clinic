Daily Wellness Habits

Evidence-Based Dental Health Tips

Brushing is only the starting point. Our clinical hygienists recommend integrating these four golden rules into your daily routine to maintain resilient enamel and clean gum tissue.

The 2x2 Minute Rule

Brush for a full 120 seconds twice daily using light circular strokes with a soft head.

Floss Before Brushing

Dislodge interdental plaque first so fluoride toothpaste can coat the newly exposed contact points.

Wait After Acidic Meals

Acid temporarily softens enamel. Wait 30 minutes before brushing; rinse with plain water immediately.

Tongue Hygiene

Over 80% of anaerobic odor-causing bacteria reside on the posterior tongue. Use a stainless scraper daily.
